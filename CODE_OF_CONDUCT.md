# Code of Conduct

MiniOps is a team project. We want working on it to be respectful and productive for everyone.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and patient, especially in code review. Review the code, not the person.
- Give feedback that is specific and actionable, and accept it in good faith.
- Share knowledge. Every member should understand the whole architecture, not only their own service.
- Keep commitments, or say early when you can't.
- Credit other people's work.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults or discriminatory language of any kind.
- Personal attacks or dismissive comments in issues, PRs or chat.
- Publishing other people's private information.
- Plagiarism, or presenting someone else's work as your own.

## Reporting

Raise concerns privately with a team member you trust, or with the course instructor. Reports are handled confidentially.

This code of conduct is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/), version 2.1.
